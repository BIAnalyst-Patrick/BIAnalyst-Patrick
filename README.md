## How I work

I work with **retailers, wholesalers and SMEs** on sales and financial
performance — the sales, margin and cash questions their owners actually decide on.

- **Find the cause, not the chart.** Every analysis starts from a decision, then
  decomposes the movement by store, category and segment to find where it sits.
- **Size every fix in gross profit.** Each recommendation carries an owner and an
  expected recovery, converted at margin, so a numbers person can argue with it.
- **Declare the limits.** Facts, hypotheses and genuinely unexplained findings are
  labelled separately. Where the data cannot answer, I say so rather than guess.

Each repo's README is written as a standalone business report: the problem, the approach, the findings, and recommendations with an owner, not just code.

**Basis** says what kind of evidence each Result is. *Simulated case study* (Revenue Decline, Monthly Management Pack, Receivables) means synthetic data, disclosed on the repo's first line, with no client outcome implied. *Dataset result* means the figures are what the analysis computed on that project's own dataset — real work on real rows, not a measured business outcome. *Illustrative target / scenario* (the 8% recovery plan, the $88K opportunity) means a sized opportunity with its arithmetic shown — **not a measured result, not realized revenue**.
